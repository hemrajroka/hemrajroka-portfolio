About Me

I am a Network and Systems Administrator with 1.5+ years of combined experience across ISP Network Operations Center (NOC) operations and enterprise IT support. Currently, I work as a Junior Network Administrator in the NOC Department at Mercantile Communication Pvt. Ltd., Nepal.

My experience includes monitoring and supporting network and IT infrastructure, troubleshooting Layer 2 and Layer 3 connectivity, working with routing protocols such as BGP and OSPF, and supporting MPLS and VPLS services. I also work with Windows Server, Ubuntu Linux, VMware, Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365 support.

I am interested in network engineering, system administration, virtualization, cloud technologies and enterprise infrastructure. I continuously develop my technical skills through practical work, troubleshooting and hands-on learning.
